## 5. Google Reviews & Q&A Strategy

### 5.1. Proactive Review System
- Generate the short GBP review link: In GBP dashboard -> *Ask for reviews* -> copy link (`https://g.page/r/.../review`).
- Provide a printed table stand with a QR code at the billing/reception counter.
- Train the clinic assistant: When a patient expresses gratitude after a painless procedure, invite them to share their honest feedback on Google.
- **Review Response Strategy**:
  - Respond to **all reviews within 24 to 48 hours**.
  - **Positive Review Template**:
    > *"Thank you [Patient Name] for your kind feedback! Dr. Kishore and our team at Sri Saraswathi Dental Clinic are delighted to hear that your treatment at our Surampatti clinic went smoothly. Wishing you continued oral health!"*

Please contact our clinic directly at [Phone] or email us at kishoreprosthodontist@gmail.com so we can address your concerns immediately."*
