## 3. Canonical URL & Redirection Standards

1. **Protocol & Host**: Enforce lowercase `https://drkishoredentist.com` without `www`.
2. **Trailing Slash Uniformity**: Strip trailing slashes (`https://drkishoredentist.com/about`, not `.../about/`).
3. **Legacy Cleanups**:
   - `/services/:slug` must 301 canonicalize or redirect to `/treatments/:slug`.
   - `/about` must NOT redirect to `/contact`. It must render the standalone `About.tsx` page.
   - Doctor slug `doctor-1` must redirect/alias to `dr-kishore-kumar`.
